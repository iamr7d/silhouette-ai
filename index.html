Tholpavakoothu, the leather shadow puppetry of Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

Shadow puppetry of Kerala and Tamil Nadu, played with leather puppets in Kerala's Palakkad, Thrissur and Malappuram districts and around Madurai. In Kerala it is dedicated to the goddess Bhadrakali, who was fighting the demon Darika when Rama killed Ravana, and so has the war played again for her.

II

Before the story, Ganapati.

A night opens with an invocation to Ganesh and to the performers' gurus, and runs until daybreak. The whole Ramayana takes twenty-one nights, between January and May.

III

The Kamba Ramayana, sung through the night.

The pulavar sings Kambar's Tamil verses and explains them in Tamil, Malayalam and Sanskrit: more than three thousand verses, learned over six to ten years.

IV

A shadow grows as it leaves the cloth.

Each figure is held flat to the cloth on two sticks, one for the body and a thinner one for an arm. Drawn back toward the lamps, its shadow swells and softens. Ravana arrives like that, and sharpens as he reaches the cloth.

V

The other side.

Where the puppeteers stand: twenty-one chirattas, halved coconut shells of oil, each with a cotton wick, on the vilakku madam, a beam about 1.3 metres up, along a stage some forty-two feet long.

VI

Two hundred figures, cut from hide.

Goat skin, once deerskin, punched so the light shows through and coloured with vegetable dyes. Some stand four feet tall, and a full telling needs 180 to 200 of them.

The figures passing now are museum pieces from several Indian traditions, in the Museu do Oriente, Lisbon.

VII

Nobody agrees how old it is.

Accounts place its beginnings anywhere between the ninth and the eighteenth century. Families of pulavars have carried it since, from the temple to Tashkent in 1979, and to the first all-women troupe in 2021.

A short chronology

  1. c. 300 CEThe earliest known mention of pavaikoothu, puppet play with leather or wooden dolls, is in the Thirukkural (kural 1020).
  2. OriginsDebated: some place the beginnings of Tholpavakoothu in the ninth or tenth century, others in the eighteenth. It was played in Bhadrakali temples even before the Kamba Ramayana became its text.
  3. 1979Krishnankutty Pulavar of Koonathara takes his troupe abroad for the first time, to Tashkent, India's only entry at a festival of puppet theatre from 55 countries.
  4. SincePadma Shri Ramachandra Pulavar leads the Kavalappara troupe in Palakkad and brings in new stories: Gandhi, Jesus Christ, and plays on drink, litter and women's safety.
  5. 2016Rajeev Pulavar receives the Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar.
  6. 2021Rajitha, the first woman in the troupe, starts Pen Pavakoothu, the first all-women Tholpavakoothu troupe.
  7. 2021Leather puppets moved by machines are shown, reported as tradition meeting technology.

The music of the night is carried by the ezhupara drum and the ilathalam cymbals; the conch, the chenda and maddalam drums and the chengila gong are kept for great moments. A troupe on the road is often eight: five to work the figures, two to sing, one to play.
