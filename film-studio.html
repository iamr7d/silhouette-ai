Silhouette.ai

A studio for shadow theatre.

An editor in the browser for Tholpavakoothu and every shadow tradition: figures, lamps, story and sound on one timeline.

Lights

Twenty-one lamps, or any light.

Clay chirattas, brass deepams, bulbs, LEDs, spots and gels, placed, dimmed and coloured over time.

Front and back

Both sides of the cloth.

Watch as the audience does, or from where the puppeteers stand, with the lamps before you.

Timeline

Every figure, keyframed.

Position, tilt, distance from the cloth and jointed limbs, with movements like walk, bow, strike and loom.

Motors

Real puppets, real lamps.

Servos and lamps on an Arduino, driven from the timeline or played live with the puppeteer's own controls.
